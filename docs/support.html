Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I sync across devices? Create an account in the app settings and enable sync.
  • Can I read offline? Yes! Downloaded translations work without an internet connection.
  • How do I change the font? Go to Settings → Theme & Fonts to choose from 12 font options.
  • Is the app free? Yes, completely free with no ads.
