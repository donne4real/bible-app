Privacy Policy

Bible in African Languages
Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bible in African Languages ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account to enable cross-device sync, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Display name (optional)

Usage Data

The App stores the following data locally on your device and syncs it to our servers when you enable sync:

  • Bookmarks and highlights
  • Reading progress and statistics
  • Notes and annotations
  • App preferences and settings (font size, theme, translation preferences)
  • Reading history

Device Information

We may collect basic device information for troubleshooting purposes, including:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash reports (if enabled)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • Cross-device sync: To synchronize your bookmarks, notes, and reading progress across your devices
  • App functionality: To save your preferences and provide a personalized reading experience
  • Improvement: To identify and fix bugs, and improve app performance

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard practices:

  • All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections
  • Data is stored in Cloudflare D1 database with encryption at rest
  • We use Cloudflare Workers for secure API processing
  • Access to your data is restricted to authenticated requests only
Your Bible reading data is private. We do not analyze, sell, or share your personal reading habits, bookmarks, or notes with any third party.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare: For hosting, database storage, and API processing. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Apple: For app distribution through the App Store and TestFlight. Apple Privacy Policy

These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

5. Bible Content

The App provides access to various Bible translations in African languages. These translations are provided by their respective organizations and publishers. The App does not modify or claim ownership of any Bible translation content.

6. Data Sharing and Selling

We do NOT:
  • Sell your personal data to anyone
  • Share your data with advertisers
  • Use your data for targeted advertising
  • Track your reading habits for analytics beyond basic app improvement

We will only disclose your information if required by law or to protect our rights.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your stored data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Correction: Update or correct your account information
  • Opt-out: Disable sync at any time (your data will remain on your device but won't sync)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
