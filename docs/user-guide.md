# Bible in African Languages — User guide

A simple guide for reading the Bible in your language.

## 1. What it is

Bible in African Languages is a free Bible reader with **22 translations** — including Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Twi, Pidgin, Swahili, Amharic, Afrikaans, Arabic, French, and more. It works **offline** (no internet needed for 19 translations) and syncs your study data across devices.

It is for personal reading and study — not investment advice. (Just kidding — it's a Bible app, not a stock app!)

## 2. Getting started

### Open the app

Go to **[bible-app.leyesapps.workers.dev](https://bible-app.leyesapps.workers.dev)** on any browser — computer or phone.

**Tip:** On a phone, use your browser's "Add to Home screen" to open it like an app.

### Choose your translation

1. Tap the **translation name** at the top (e.g., "World English Bible")
2. Pick from the list of 22 translations
3. Your choice is saved automatically

## 3. Reading

### Navigate chapters

- **Book picker** — tap the book name at the top to choose any book, chapter, or verse
- **Swipe** — swipe left/right on mobile to go to next/previous chapter
- **Keyboard** — use arrow keys on desktop
- **Search** — type a reference like "John 3:16" or "Gen 1" to jump directly

### Daily Verse

Every day shows a featured verse at the top of the home page. There are 365 unique verses — one for each day of the year.

### Reading Plans

Tap the **menu** (☰) → **Reading Plans** to choose from:

| Plan | Pace |
|---|---|
| New Testament in 30 Days | ~9 chapters/day |
| Psalms in 30 Days | 5 psalms/day |
| Proverbs in 30 Days | 1 chapter/day |
| Chronological in 365 Days | Full Bible in historical order |

Your progress is tracked automatically.

## 4. Study tools

### Highlights

1. Tap and hold a verse
2. Choose a **color** (5 options) and **style** (background or underline)
3. Highlights persist across translations (the same verse stays highlighted even if you switch languages)

### Notes

1. Tap and hold a verse
2. Tap the **note icon**
3. Type your reflection — saved automatically

### Bookmarks

1. Tap the **bookmark icon** (🔖) in the chapter header
2. Your place is saved — tap again to remove

### Cross-references

1. Tap a verse number
2. Related verses appear — tap any to jump to it

### Export / Import

Back up all your study data (highlights, notes, bookmarks) as a JSON file:
1. Menu (☰) → **Export/Import**
2. **Export** downloads a JSON backup
3. **Import** restores from a backup file

## 5. Cloud sync (optional)

Sync your highlights, notes, and bookmarks across devices:

1. Menu (☰) → **Sync**
2. **Create an account** (email + password)
3. Your data syncs automatically:
   - **Auto-push** — changes upload 3 seconds after you make them
   - **Auto-pull** — changes download when you open the app or every 5 minutes
4. Manual **Push** and **Pull** buttons are also available

Your data is encrypted and stored on Cloudflare's edge network. Sync is completely optional — the app works 100% offline without it.

## 6. Reading statistics

Menu (☰) → **Reading Stats** shows:

- **Current streak** — consecutive days of reading
- **Longest streak** — your best run
- **Total days** — how many days you've read
- **Time spent** — total reading time
- **Book progress** — visual bars for all 66 books
- **Activity log** — recent reading sessions with timestamps

## 7. Customization

### Fonts (12 options)

Menu (☰) → **Settings** → **Font**:

- **Serif:** Playfair Display, Literata, Merriweather, Noto Serif, Source Serif 4, Crimson Text, EB Garamond, Lora, Vollkorn, Spectral
- **Sans-serif:** Inter
- **Monospace:** JetBrains Mono

### Themes (4 options)

- **Sepia** — warm, easy on the eyes (default)
- **Light** — clean white
- **Dark** — dark gray background
- **OLED Charcoal** — pure black for OLED screens

Themes auto-switch with your system's light/dark preference.

### Text size

Adjust from small to 5XL in Settings.

### Zen mode

Hides all UI chrome for distraction-free reading. Toggle in Settings.

## 8. Sharing

### Share a verse

1. Tap and hold a verse
2. Tap the **share icon**
3. Choose: **WhatsApp**, **Twitter**, **Telegram**, **Facebook**, or **Copy link**

### Verse card

1. Tap and hold a verse
2. Tap **Share Card**
3. Choose a background and style
4. Download as an image or share directly

## 9. Comparison view

Read two translations side by side:

1. Tap the **comparison icon** (two columns) in the header
2. Choose a second translation
3. View **side-by-side** or **interlinear** (verse-by-verse) layout

## 10. Good to know

- **Works offline** — 19 translations are bundled; no internet needed
- **3 remote translations** (NLT, AMP, NIV) require internet
- **Your data stays yours** — stored locally; sync is optional
- **No ads, no login required** — the app is free and open source
- **Privacy policy:** [donne4real.github.io/bible-app/](https://donne4real.github.io/bible-app/)
